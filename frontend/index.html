How trust is handled

What the AI can and cannot tell you

Confidence is not certainty

A softmax score is the model's preference among the classes it was trained on. It is not the probability that the identification is correct, and it is not a conservation record.

It abstains on weak evidence

Small, dark, blurry or out-of-distribution photos are rejected before classification. Close top-two scores and implausible locations trigger “needs review” instead of a confident answer.

Missing data stays missing

Tourism timings, fees, contacts and accessibility notes are only shown when a source recorded them. Otherwise the page says “Not verified” and invites a sourced correction.

Photograph something interesting?

Run a quality-checked identification. You can keep the result without saving it, or consent to store it as a pending observation.
