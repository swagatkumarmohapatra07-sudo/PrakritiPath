How to read confidence

These labels are deliberately cautious.

High for this model (≥ 80%) — clears the threshold, but still requires human confirmation.
Moderate (55–79%) — plausible, but look-alike species remain possible.
Low (< 55%) — the app abstains and asks for review rather than auto-accepting.
